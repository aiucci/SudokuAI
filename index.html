Tap a cell, then type 1–9. Arrow keys move, N toggles notes. In notes mode a digit removes or restores a mark, including the auto ones.

What each control does

1–9
Writes that digit in the selected cell. A filled cell is never overwritten — clear it with Erase first.
Erase
Clears the selected cell: a wrong number, its notes, and any candidates you crossed off. It stays disabled on givens and on numbers that are already correct.
Notes
Switches digit taps between filling cells and editing pencil marks.
Auto notes
Shows every empty cell's legal candidates and keeps them in step with the board. In Notes mode you can cross a candidate off — or put it back — to record a deduction. Cells left with one candidate are tinted green, those with two are tinted blue.
Check
Marks any cells that disagree with the solution.
Solve
Fills in the whole solution and ends the puzzle. Options can animate it cell by cell instead of revealing it at once.
Time
Time spent on this puzzle. It stops when you solve or reveal it.
Mistakes
The X marks in the top right turn red for each wrong number entered. A third mistake ends the game.
Remaining
How many cells are still empty, counting down as you go. Hidden until you turn it on in Options.
Clues
How many cells came pre-filled. Those are the bold givens, and they cannot be edited. Hidden until you turn it on in Options.
New puzzle
Asks for a difficulty — Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert — then generates a puzzle at that level and restarts the timer. Cancel keeps the current game.
Options
Opens the settings panel: switch off the highlighting of the selected number, row, column and box, show the clues counter or the remaining cell count, mute the placement sounds, or animate Solve.
Keyboard
Arrow keys move, 1–9 enters a digit, Backspace / Delete / 0 clears, N toggles Notes, Esc closes this help.
