What each control does

1–9
Writes that digit in the selected cell. Givens and numbers that are already correct are fixed; a wrong digit turns red on the spot, counts as a mistake, and can simply be typed over with a new one.
Notes
Switches digit taps between filling cells and editing pencil marks. It greys out once the game is over or solved.
Auto notes
Shows every empty cell's legal candidates and keeps them in step with the board. In Notes mode you can cross a candidate off — or put it back — to record a deduction. Cells left with one candidate are tinted green, those with two are tinted blue. Like Notes, it greys out once the game is over or solved.
Solve
Fills in the whole solution and ends the puzzle. Options can animate it cell by cell instead of revealing it at once. It greys out once the game is over.
Time
Shown beside the mistake marks in the top right. It stops when you solve or reveal the puzzle, and Options can hide it.
Difficulty
The level of the current puzzle, shown between the clock and the mistake marks. Options can hide it.
Mistakes
The X marks in the top right turn red for each wrong number entered. A third mistake ends the game.
Remaining
How many cells are still empty, counting down as you go. Hidden until you turn it on in Options.
Clues
How many cells came pre-filled. Those are the bold givens, and they cannot be edited. Hidden until you turn it on in Options.
New puzzle
Asks for a difficulty — Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert — then generates a puzzle at that level and restarts the timer. Cancel keeps the current game.
Options
Opens the settings panel: turn the row, column and box highlighting on or off (it starts off), do the same for the cells already holding the selected number (they start highlighted), show or hide the time and the difficulty level (both start shown), show the clues counter or the remaining cell count, mute the placement sounds, animate Solve, or switch to dark mode.
Keyboard
Arrow keys move, 1–9 enters a digit, N toggles Notes, Esc closes this help.
